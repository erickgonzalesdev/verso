Issue 01 · Sample · plate rhythm

Inset Float Left

Every image is a passage. Order alone assigns the beat: inset, float right, float left — then the loop. Freeform stages take any PNG shape; captions always sit under the plate.

Intervening paragraphs and headings do not reset the count — only section elements do. Put wrap copy in .passage__body so it flows beside and under floats.

Inset → float right → float left → repeat. Same bones every issue. Plate rhythm

After an inset, ordinary copy continues in the measure. The next section.passage floats right — no extra classes required.

How the cycle works

Among siblings of main.article-body, every section.passage is numbered by CSS :nth-of-type:

  • 1, 4, 7… — inset
  • 2, 5, 8… — float right
  • 3, 6, 9… — float left

Text in passage__body wraps the plate on wide screens and continues under it. Empty slots are valid — ship layout before the scan lands. Keep float bodies long enough to clear tall frames.

Prefer freeform .plate__stage for cutouts; use .plate__frame only when you need a rectangular crop.

Third passage of the cycle. After this, the next section is inset again — the loop is the whole framework. Caption stack is always kicker → title → credit under the image.

Second cycle

Same markup, new pictures. Vary crop and content; keep the skeleton identical.

Optional .plate__frame with aspect-ratio for photos that should crop. Float body still wraps under the figure.

Escapes when needed: passage--inset, passage--float-right, passage--float-left, passage--span, passage--fill. Prefer rare.

End of the second cycle. Add more passages as the piece needs; they keep stepping through the same three beats.

Span escape

A span is still a section, so it advances the counter. Place it with awareness of the next beat.
