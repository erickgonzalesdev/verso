Impact moments reuse the Evangelion title card — stacked series, mono label left, title flush right. The plate rhythm still cycles; plain headers stay quiet.

Ordinary paragraphs stay plain. When a beat needs emphasis, wrap a section.passage around the plate and put a title-card title-card--inline in .passage__body so they travel together.
